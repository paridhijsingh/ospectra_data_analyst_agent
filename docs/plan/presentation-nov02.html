Where leaks happen in home care

Documentation delays, coding / OASIS errors, missed filing windows, authorization mismatches — most are created at steps 1–3, long before a payer says no.

Our wedge lives at step 8

$190k earned, not collected. One $40k reimbursement 14 days late = $40k short against $105k pre-obligation cash. The agent's job: warn at steps 4–7, before step 8 breaks.

RCM = every financial step from intake to cash5 / 11

Research · Candid Health

Candid Health: rebuild the billing system around payer rules — don't layer on top

What it is

  • The billing system of record — the financial counterpart to the EHR: registration, eligibility, claims, remits, denials, A/R, reporting
  • A configurable, payer-aware rules engine checks + corrects claims before submission
  • API-first, EHR-integrated; now adding an agentic layer over Candid + legacy billing systems
  • Company-reported: 200+ provider orgs, ~$12B claim volume this year; $120M Series D, July 2026

The two metrics it runs on

Net collection rate
% of collectible payer revenue a provider actually receives

Touchless Claim Rate
% of claims submitted + finalized with no human touch

Fit gap — say it first: Candid's customers are medical groups + digital health. No public evidence it handles HCBA waiver billing, EVV reconciliation, or OASIS-driven home-care claims. Pricing is not published. Today it is a reference model, not a recommendation.
Sources: candidhealth.com · CEO interview, Healthcare AI Guy · Pulse2 Series D report6 / 11
